How to play

  1. Roll the steel marble into a pocket of your color. First to 5 wins.
  2. Red plays from the left end and Blue from the right. Each end has one red pocket and one blue pocket, each with a peg standing guard.
  3. Hold a lever and that corner of the board sinks, deeper the longer you hold. The marble rolls toward it: toward you, and to that side. Let go and the corner rises back up. Tap to hold it where it is.
  4. Either pocket of your color scores, the one at your end or the one at theirs. The other color’s pocket sits right beside yours, so don’t pull the marble into it. You both tilt the same board: press to pull the marble back when it’s heading the wrong way.
  5. Push meter: a gentle press costs nothing. Holding a lever all the way down uses up your push. When it runs out, let go of both levers and it refills.
  6. After each point the marble goes back to the middle, and a 3·2·1 countdown starts the next one.

Controls

  • Same phoneLay the phone flat between you. Each player presses and holds the two pads at their own end.
  • Online, phoneHeld upright, your two pads sit side by side along the bottom. On its side, they stack on the left: top and bottom.
  • ComputerHold A or ← for your left lever, D or → for your right.

From Sandy’s workshop

Sandy Kraemer invented On Guard. Parker Brothers licensed and published it as a tilting game for 2 to 4 players. He went on to invent more games, including Best Life.

This web version is for 2 players, in red and blue.
