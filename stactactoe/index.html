How to play

  1. Goal. Align 3 spheres of your color in a vertical, horizontal, or diagonal row at any height. The first player to win 3 rounds takes the match.
  2. Phase 1, Place. Yellow and Orange have 5 spheres each. Take turns putting one into any of the 3 columns, on the platform or on top of a stack.
  3. Phase 2, Stack Play. Once all 10 spheres are down, pick any sphere of your color, on top or buried, and move that sphere plus everything stacked above it to another column. Stacks have no height limit.
  4. Traps. If your move gives only your opponent 3 in a row, you lose the round. If it makes 3 in a row for both players at once, the round is a draw.
Touch / mouse
Tap a column to place. In Stack Play, tap or drag one of your spheres, then tap or release on another column.
Keyboard
1 2 3 pick a column (press again or use ↑ ↓ to step through buried spheres). Esc puts a lifted stack back. M mutes.
