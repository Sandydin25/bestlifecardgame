Meet the Creator

Sandy F. Kraemer

Lawyer, author, inventor, lifelong game player, and the mind behind Best Life.

Sandy Kraemer has spent his life helping people get the most out of theirs. After studying at Stanford and earning his law degree at the University of Colorado, he built a long career as an estate-planning attorney in Colorado Springs, helping thousands of families plan for the future.

Along the way he wrote the books on it, including the 60 Minute Estate Planner, and later How To Ask, his guide to asking for what you want. It’s no accident that in Best Life, asking is how you win.

Sandy has given a great deal back to his community, too. He served on the University of Colorado Board of Regents, including a term as its chairman, and the library at the University of Colorado Colorado Springs carries his family’s name: the Kraemer Family Library.

And Sandy has always loved games. He has played pinball at the Penny Arcade in Manitou Springs since he was a kid, he has been inventing games for decades, and the very first one he invented was licensed to Parker Brothers. Best Life is his newest, a game about the things that make a good life: family, friends, goals, humor, and a little wisdom on every card.

How to Play

Ask to win!

2 to 6 players, ages 7 to 107. If you’ve ever played Go Fish, you already know the feeling. Here’s how it goes, straight from the rules card.

1

The cards

52 cards includes 12 sets of 4 BEST LIFE CARDS such as 4 MONEY or 4 FOOD (each card with a different Connecting Point) plus 4 ACTION CARDS. Read Connecting Points only for wit and wisdom.

12 sets × 4 cards + 4 Action cards = 52.

2

Object of the game

Win the most sets of BEST LIFE CARDS, such as 4 MONEY or 4 FOOD CARDS.

Get all four of a kind and the set is yours.

3

The deal

Any player can be dealer. Dealer shuffles all cards. Dealer then deals 7 cards face down to each player if 2–4 players, 5 cards each for 5–6 players. Remaining deck is put face down to make the Help Stack. Organize cards in hand.

Pick a number of players and watch the deal.

4

Play

Player to dealer’s left looks directly at any player and asks that player for help collecting BEST LIFE CARDS of a specific set by saying “Help me with…”. For example “Help me with MONEY”.

Eye contact is part of the game!
